Fakemon Taster

I was recently given 2 neat Fakemon based on short prompts I made. This one being the dual type fire/water and the word "pop". This monster is kind of slimy, and its colors changed based on what it eats, which is then expelled via popping bubbles on its skin and smoke/mist from its nose. A very simple design to go with a cute concept. (In the top left corner is my own original character who I have yet to introduce, drew him first for warm up)
