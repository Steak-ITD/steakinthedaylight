This took me nine and a half hours total! Mainly in Krita, where I did all sorts of stuff like cleaning the traditional sketch lines and isolating them from the white paper, playing with layer modes for the colors, and filter and selection tools for the photo backgrounds. It was worth it, but perhaps I should take more breaks in between.

This pairing is Julia/Julius, who are twins. I wrote this about them below:

Lately I have been imagining Julia/Julius (Fire Emblem Genealogy of the Holy War) in a universe where they could have had a longer sibling relationship. They're quite opposite to each other, the way I imagine their personalities, but they stick together like magnets.
I think of Julia as autistic, shy, somewhat closed off and quiet. But she isn't anxious, at least not without reason. While Julius is outgoing, good with people, both street and book smart. He appears more confident outwardly.
These twins are different in their mannerisms but they're still seen in proximity with each other often. They have a high amount of patience with each other. Julius helps Julia navigate social situations she is unfamiliar with, while she helps him keep his emotions and temper in check.
As kids they were nigh inseparable. They would have a kind of silent twin language, almost as if reading each others' minds. They would sleep together in the same bed, hugging each other close, relishing in the body warmth. As they get older and their individual personalities develop, they still remain close. Of course not without their arguments and issues though, as two differing temperments are bound to do.
In a romantic scenario: I think they are adept at hiding and waiting for moments to act on their feelings. I doubt anyone would suspect anything of them despite their closeness, but they're still careful most of the time. In a quiet corner of a park, in their bedrooms when their parents are gone, they could share kisses, hands and lips moving all over their bodies. Leaving marks where only they can see.
