Airlines the Castom

I adopted yet another fusion. It's a mix of Castform and Rotom. At the nehest of my friend, I have named it Airlines. Uh, bio some other time!

My goal in making this digital piece was to figure out what kind of method and style I want to stick with. Top left linework used a more aliased, pixely pen, G-Pen, and I edited it with CSP Vector layer tools. The coloring was flatter, using fill and blender tools. I think this is mostly how I did it in the past.

Bottom right, I used a custom brush, the name escapes me right now... and I made multiple iterations of the lines, which come together to form the general shape. Not sure how to describe that process. The coloring, I used the custom brush Oil Knife, and switched colors back and forth to blend them.

I think I prefer the bottom right one. It feels like it has more "personality" and potential to feel more loose and free, whereas the top left feels too rigid and stiff, but cleaner. I don't know if either style is going to be fun for more complex shapes and figures.
