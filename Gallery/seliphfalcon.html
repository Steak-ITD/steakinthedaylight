Seliph Falcon

In my FE4 Sphinxverses, the Chalphys are falcons. Oh no, I have a bulleted list for this boy.

  1. First sketch, struggled with upper leg placement cause it was hard to tell with the photo I referenced. Also still trying to figure how how I want to draw human faces. This one's not really finished.
  2. Second sketch, came out easier and more visually pleasing. He caught a smaller bird.
  3. Wing pattern. Dots are uniform towards the bottom and random towards the top.
  4. He has sharp teeth for catching prey. Falcons use their beaks for tearing meat rather than their talons, and since he has a human face this wouldn't work, so I gave him sharp teeth.

I didn't get around to displaying this but he does have bird ears, covered outward by feathers.
