Title + Description from "Lollipop" by Mika

Tonight the treat was a heart shaped lollipop with white and purple swirled stripes. It was wrapped with a red bow. Lemres took it from his butler wordlessly. There was no longer a need for words. He laid down on his bed and started to unwrap the treat as his butler looms over, unwrapping him all the same.

The lollipop tastes like passion fruit. Lemres hadn't expected that. It was a good flavor. He sucks it lightly. His butler does the same to him, and he moans. He squeezes his eyes shut tighter.

Focus on the lollipop.

It's sweet, hard, but seems to melt the longer he has it in his mouth. It feels nice… he wondered what ingredients were in it… passion fruit… such an interesting choice…

He's being touched down there. He can't stop from tensing, knowing the hardest part is to come. His butler doesn't seem to mind.

Don't worry… just stay focused on the lollipop.

It's tasty, isn't it? Tasty. Sweet. Unique. Pretty. Lovely. Shaped like a heart. A sweet heart, just like you.

Lemres grits his teeth. He bites down. The heart breaks in his mouth. Sharp edges scrap his tongue. It hurts.

Sweet boy, don't worry. Just focus. Chew.

It crumbles in his mouth. The crunches satisfy him. It dissolves in his mouth and he swallows. It really is nice. It's never enough though. It still hurts. But can he really complain? At least when he does this, he's loved. Sweetly, like his treat.
