Notes

I ended up writing enough about these two to fill a doc page. It was pretty fun, I like imagining this dynamic.

General

They're quite opposite to each other, the way I imagine their personalities, but they stick together like magnets. I think of Julia as autistic, shy, somewhat closed off and quiet. But she isn't anxious, at least not without reason. While Julius is outgoing, good with people, both street and book smart. He appears more confident outwardly. These twins are different in their mannerisms but they're still seen in proximity with each other often. They have a high amount of patience with each other. Julius helps Julia navigate social situations she is unfamiliar with, while she helps him keep his emotions and temper in check. As kids they were nigh inseparable. They would have a kind of silent twin language, almost as if reading each others' minds. They would sleep together in the same bed, hugging each other close, relishing in the body warmth. As they get older and their individual personalities develop, they still remain close. Of course not without their arguments and issues though, as two differing temperaments are bound to do.


Julius

He's a people person, outwardly. At least, he is good with people. He has a lot of charisma, it draws others to him. He's intelligent as well. He can figure out what sorts of things will attract other's attention, what they would like to see and hear... But he also figures what gets under one's skin. What makes them tick. This is particularly useful for politics. It's hard to say he really cares that much for others, despite how well he can gel with them. There are, of course, some people he holds dear. In reality he is easily prone to obsession. If he can't figure something out he'll do anything to solve it. He doesn't rest until his curiosity is sated completely. He can become consumed in his feelings, as well... He feels them intensely and externally, which can surprise or scare others. Loptous amplifies this personality, twists and warps it for his own purposes. It's hard to tell where Julius begins and ends, how conscious he is, how much he wants what is happening or not. Peace: He is definitely an edgelord. Likes blood and gore, scary movies, creepy creatures like rats and bugs. He also likes to do weird things to annoy his sister.


Julia

She's very shy. Not good with people, not even sure how to approach others, and tends to freeze when approach. At times she wishes she was invisible, unable to be perceived. Simply wants to exist in the air, in the sounds around her. She plays with her hands a lot: fiddling with her fingers, flicking them in the air as if imitating a spell. She would have fidget rings as well. She also feels emotions intensely, but is not good at (or sometimes not interested in) expressing them outwardly. Struggles to put her feelings into words and sentences that "make sense". She seems to have some level of clairvoyance where she can detect danger, or high power, or something. She enjoys soft things in general. Pastel colors, slow music, particularly from radios. Furry mammals, long dresses, ballet... Things that feel and look gentle to her. If she has a phase, it would probably be emo to go with her brother's goth.


Romance

I think they are adept at hiding and waiting for moments to act on their feelings. I doubt anyone would suspect anything of them despite their closeness, but they're still careful most of the time. In a quiet corner of a park, in their bedrooms when their parents are gone, they could share kisses, hands and lips moving all over their bodies. Leaving marks where only they can see. Thinking about the difference in awareness of incest taboo between Ephraim/Eirika and Julius/Julia: The former are unaware there is even an issue for quite some time, and would be confused about what's so wrong being that kind of close to your sibling. Eventually they accept that they're not meant to be "out", but it's a little tricky when both prince and princess are courted by others... The latter learn of this taboo sooner, but find it doesn't bother them. If anything the fact they need to hide makes it more exciting. They like having this sort of thing just for themselves. Also, would it be so bad if, maybe, their other siblings could join them? Well, not right away, it's risky... Perversion Now, above I mentioned that Julia and Julius might want to involve their other two siblings in their, mhem, relationship. And so, I'm getting hot and bothered thinking about this particular scenario, where Seliph catches them after the act. While out with a group of friends, the twins snuck away somewhere quiet, and had a rendezvous together. While they come back, trying to fix themselves up as if nothing happened, they're seen and heard by their older sibling. He scrutinizes them, and mentions he had suspicions about their activities. Seliph is about to/is already chastising them, but then Julius tries to divert attention by saying how Seliph must have felt "left out" by their antics. Which appears to irritate him more... Julia tries to stop him, but not very hard, and she is blushing more and more. Julius enjoys pushing Seliph's buttons... He half excepts it when he grabs his wrists and holds him against the wall, glaring at him with heavy breaths. "I really wish you wouldn't tease me like that," he says. Julia gets behind Seliph, ostensibly trying to pull him back, but again not very hard. Then Julius says "Why don't you show me how you feel, Big Brother?" Last part whispered in his ear. This compels Seliph to crash his lips against Julius' and they roughly make out. Julia watches from behind in awe. And they all give each other handjobs and kisses in the alleyway just out of sight. No one catches them, I imagine they would have been at a street fair or carnival or something at night. So they get some quality time together!
