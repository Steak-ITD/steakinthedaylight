How things changed.

It was strange for Caeda to adjust to these new feelings, these new thoughts. But it wasn't quite difficult either. She had been an alpha, and she had the impulses that came with that. But she hadn't claimed any omegas. It never felt right to her, or maybe she hadn't met the right person. So, she hadn't been in a role of being in charge like that.

And now, she was the one who was claimed. Her mind had rejected it; made her panic when she was slathered in Tiki's scent, made her shiver when she touched her mark. Over time, that sense of fear dulled from a shriek into a whisper, something she could let go through her. She could take comfort in being protected instead.

To Caeda's slight surprise, Tiki had more trouble adjusting to owning her. She hadn't asked for specifics about her past, but she knew it had been harsh, worn on her young soul, which lead her to ... even still, Caeda couldn't bring herself to resent Tiki, not fully. A part of her wanted to, still feeling hurt by what happened, but she had always been an empathetic person first and foremost, not believing bitterness would solve anything.

They had an unusual relationship, after all. Everyone could tell how strange it was when they went out in public. From the clashing scents, to the scars of the mating bite on Caeda's shoulder. Tiki was unsure how she should act, about the "right" way to present herself and her claim. Most were hesitant to truly bring it up, knowing how powerful a divine dragon was.

Eventually, as they grew more used to each other, the pair fell into a rhythm that suit them. They were hardly ever seen apart, both becoming rather clingy. Tiki's heats began triggering Caeda's ruts. The dragon still dominated during mating, but she became better at monitoring her strength and making sure she and her woman were satisfied.

Tiki continued to whisper in Caeda's ear, in the night as they settle for bed. Her woman's name, her claim, her desire to keep her safe.
