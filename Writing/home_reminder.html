Past Child Abuse

Sig noticed them while he and Lemres were changing clothes together one night.

There were many red and pink marks dashed throughout the skin on his back. They were faded, but still visible from only a few feet away.

"Your back... It's covered in scars," Sig said, with a hint of worry.

"Oh, those..." Lemres turned his head a bit to look back. "They're burns. I got them from cooking."

"Burns on your back from cooking?"

"Mmhmm," Lemres replies, rather quickly, but then falls silent. Something crosses his mind, before he knows it.

"I don't want to have to do this, " Father's voice was stern and low. "But you need to learn to obey. This is important for your future. Do you understand?"

Lemres stares at the ground, hands locked tightly together, trying not to cry. He still sniffles, scared of what's to come.

"I see you have nothing to say. Then, we'll continue. You there, behind him. Please make sure he remembers."

The butler sighs, then comes around. They chant something simple. Lemres doesn't hear the words, but he hears the flames a second before they hit.

"It was a long time ago. I was quite clumsy, back then."
