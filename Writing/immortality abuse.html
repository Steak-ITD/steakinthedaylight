Immortality Abuse

Tails finds out something concerning about himself against his will.

Characters
Miles Prower - the lab rat
Dr. Eggman - the scientist
Sonic the Hedgehog - also here
Info
Universe :: Classic Sonic
Genre :: Sci Fi?
Ships :: None
Warnings

Graphic Violence, Major Character Death multiple times

Notes

A story based on the concepts "Tails Abuse" and "Tails Can't Die". I'm surprised that these haven't been combined yet.

Before he opens his eyes, Tails can tell he's in a bright, sterile room, the lights shining through his eyelids. Once he does, he sees the walls are a bright grey, making the light reflect more. His nose tells him its metallic, and the temperature matches the cold feeling of the small space.

He barely remembers being here. Most everything is blank before he fell asleep. He strains trying to recall, just before, he was somewhere similar, with...

"Good morning!" Eggman has a wider than usual smile. That's all Tails sees before an axe is dropped straight in the middle of his head.

The pain is instant and he can do nothing but scream. He thinks he is crying too, but he isn't sure. His breaths are heavy too, he's panicking, he can't think straight at all! There's-! An axe!?

The axe is pulled out quickly, taking fur, blood, and what is probably bone with it. Then it's swung at him again. Of course, Tails can't react in time. How could he? His head! And now his left eye, chopped partly in half. No, what else could he do now but collapse, consciousness leaving-

Tails wakes up feeling... Average. He can tell he's not home based on the scent, but he's definitely indoors. It's not a very nice place though. It's bright and metallic.

Then a particular metal hits his nose. He looks down, and there's... Blood! And orange and white fur. His fur.

Tails gasps and takes a look at his own body. He looks and feels perfectly fine. He touches his sides to make sure. No pain. But he knew that fur was his. So how was he-

"Welcome back, fox!" A loud voice he recognized came from across the room. Tails quickly looked over to see Eggman grinning at his growing confusion.

"What did you do to me? Wh-why am I here?" Tails tries to yell angrily, but the shake in his voice reveals his fear.

"So you really don't remember at all. Interesting," The doctor takes out a clipboard and writes something down. Then he pulls out a remote and clicks a button on it. "Look to your left. That should enlighten you."

Tails does as he's told. There's a simple tv screen hanging from the walls. The footage plays what looks like one of Eggman's robot facilities. It showed Sonic and Tails fighting robots of various creatures. Nothing out of the ordinary.

Until one moment, not too far in. The two are crossing over factory machines, trying to get through as fast as possible. But it's tricky to get through all the gears, flatteners, saws...

Tails watches as one of his tails is caught in a spinning gear. He watches Sonic run ahead, unaware of what happened now far behind him. He watches his own face change, knowing what would happen next.

He covers his eyes and folds his ears as he tries to block the sound of his own screeching.

Slowly moving his hands, he came to the realization that this made no sense. He remembered going through that facility with Sonic. But he didn't remember getting caught in those gears, and certainly not being crushed. He didn't look any worse for wear either. How could that be possible? "What's going on? How am I still here?"

"That's what I want to know!" Eggman says. "It makes no sense. I've definitely killed you 4 more times, yet somehow you come back as if nothing happened. Another anomaly you're related to," he scribbles more in his notes. "When I saw such a thing, I had to know more. Experiment with the scientific method, and all. I'm sure an inquisitive mind such as yours will understand?"

Tails looked at the doctor like he was going to vomit. "... No, I... Where is Sonic?"

"No doubt searching for you right now, and that pesky rat will stop at nothing, so I have a limited time here. Now if you would kindly cooperate..." He started walking towards the frightened fox, who backed away in turn.

"No! Get away-" he felt a pinch in his neck from behind. He turned to find another robot had snuck behind him and injected him with something from a needle.

"I have lots of questions. Things I want to try. It's quite exciting, don't you think?" The last thing Tails heard was laughter as his hearing and sight began to fade. He tried hard to fight it, but his body was going numb, he couldn't keep his head up...

Tails wakes up, slowly and fearfully, immediately recognizing he was not in his home, not anywhere familiar. It was a bright metallic room...

 

... That's right, he did know this room at this point. He remembered why he was here: trapped, used as a lab rat for a sick man's "experiment". He remembered that video. He should be dead. He had been dead. Yet, when the fox looked himself over, felt his body, he felt no damage internally nor externally. He really was just fine.

"Hey," Tails called out, knowing who would respond.

Indeed, that tv screen turned on, showing the doctor examining... What looked like Tails' own arm, detatched with dried blood and bone pocking out where it would have attached to the shoulder. "Good evening! Apologies I can't give you my full focus right now. Just working on a little project, but I'm nearly done. I think you'll find it gives you much to reflect on." Eggman grinned at his apparent hint.

Tails had an idea of what it meant, but he had other questions himself. "What exactly did you find out about me?"

"I was right, wasn't I? You're just as inquisitive as I am."

"It's about my own body! My life! Of course I'm curious!" Tails tried not to sound on the verge of tears even as they begin forming at his eyes.

"Oh I don't know. I could tell you something, but how do I know you won't end up using it against me?"

"W-w-why would- how-" he was interrupted by the sound of a crash and a shake.

"He's here already!? Drat, I should have expected-" a louder crash was heard. Tails could feel the rumbles of activity outside whereever he was. For the first time in a while, he smiled hopefully.

"Sonic! I'm here!" He ran up to the wall closest to the noise, banging against it with his fists. "Can you hear me? In here!"

"Quiet you! I need to-" Eggman was cut off by the loudest crash of all coming directly from the wall Tails leaned against. It gives way, making the monitor fall to the ground and break apart. Sonic bursts into the room, taking a quick look around and immediately laying his eyes on his friend.

No words needed to be exchanged; Tails is already running in tears to hug him. He jumps into open arms.

"It's ok, I'm here, I'm here," Sonic tells Tails softly as he cries. "Let's go. Somewhere safer."

Sniffling, Tails looks up at Sonic. "... Wait. Not yet. There's... something I need to find. I'll tell you later, but I need to confirm some things."

Sonic looked back with brief confusion, but nodded. "Okay, but I'm not letting you out of my sight this time."

After a longer trek back to a more quiet area, Tails stared at the documents in his hands. He could comprehend the notes, but he didn't want to believe them.

"You say you can't die, huh. If you weren't sitting here besides me, I'd find it hard to believe." Sonic said rather solemnly. "How do you feel?"

"It's... A lot. I have so many questions myself. Some of which Eggman seems to have answered here," Tails laughs darkly. "Part of me wishes I never found out at all." He puts the paper down, and stares at nothing in particular. "What does this mean? Why me?"

Sonic walks behind him and puts a hand on his friend's back. "For what it's worth, I'm glad you're still here. And I'll make sure you don't have to deal with this alone."

"Thank you... I think... I just want to sleep now, for real."
