A Dream of Tiki: Short Conceptual Rom Hack

Download Here (1.3)

One night I had a rather interesting dream.
During the war against the earth dragons, Naga is attacked personally in a surprise ambush. She is unprepared to fight as she has baby Tiki with her. Tiki is raised by the earth dragons and fights for them. I remember this version of Tiki appearing in FEH and expressing confusion over the survival of Anri's kin.
As a dream it doesn't make much sense, but the basic idea intrigued me. What if Tiki were raised by an earth dragon? I thought about it more and came up with some ideas for how that would go. Hopefully this makes slightly more sense.

Let's say the roles Naga gives Gotoh and Medeus are swapped. Gotoh guards the Dragon Altar and Medeus watches Tiki in the Ice Temple. They both watch over humanity. 

Reaching the same conclusion about the foolishness and cruelty of humanity, Medeus wakes Tiki. He shows her the horrible treatment of their kind.

Tiki is terrified and confused by what she is seeing. She doesn't know what to think... She wants to think there are humans that aren't like this, but this is all she has seen. However she doesn't share Medeus' desire to hurt the humans back.

She has some interest in his idea of a manakete empire. Maybe they could live peacefully on their own like this, away from humans and their abuse... Things don't work out that way...

There's a couple scenarios I had in mind for where this could go from here. Speculative and maybe a bit delusional.

1. Tiki doesn't want any part of the War of Liberation. Turns out she is not the only one. She steps up as a leader of sorts amongst this group, and they leave the continent. Their goal is to regrow dragon society, albeit in a smaller, more isolated form. Not sure where they would end up going. Probably a place separate from the three continents we know of this world. I also don't think this would affect the stories of those continents very heavily, but it would probably affect how the plot of Awakening goes. I will have to think on it more.
 
2. Reluctantly, Tiki stays and takes part in the war. She slowly becomes bitter watching her comrades and humans kill each other. Medeus' defeat is her breaking point. As Anri's appearance was sudden, so too is hers, and she is able to catch him by surprise and defeat him. This leaves both sides in shambles, and Archanea ravaged. She leaves it as such, refusing to help or harm any further.

The degeneration: I don't think this would happen fully for Tiki because she is already a manakete at a young age. However due to her early waking, immense power, and the Binding Shield being incomplete, she has some symptoms of it. Most commonly, boughts of rage brought on by minor things or seemingly nothing at all. She also has issues with fatigue.

In scenario 1 where I imagine the events of SD and MotE still happen, the Binding Shield is restored eventually, and she feels that relief. I assume she knows about the shield, and it's been long enough that she would be curious about how things have changed. 

She learns about the conclusion of the War of Liberation, and the two most recent wars. There are few of her kind left on Archanea, but they are no longer treated like insects as before. It nearly brings her to tears.

In scenario 2, the whereabouts of the Fire Emblem and the jewels needed to complete it are unknown. Tiki hides away and puts herself into a deep sleep, hoping to stave off her degeneration as long as possible. Maybe even wake up in a world where she will feel less apathetic.

Scenario 2 is the one I decided to create a rom hack about, as a way to illustrate the idea. It is a very short one chapter hack made with FEBuilderGBA within a couple days-a week. I wanted to see if this program was something I could actually use and, with a few hiccups, turns out I can. It's an easy hack, as this is more of a presentation than a true game, I think. There shouldn't be any trouble completing it. The difficulties don't change anything and I didn't know how to disable them, so don't worry about it. Please patch it to a USA/Australia copy of The Sacred Stones.
